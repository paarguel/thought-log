# What's new

Changes people using Thought Record will notice, newest first. Store review
status lives in the release checklists under [docs/](docs/).

## 1.1.1 (iOS only) — November 2026

- No change to the app. This version exists so the App Store page can show
  the new description and screenshots.

## 1.1.0 — November 2026

### New

- **Emotion check-in.** The first screen now asks whether you are writing
  down a thought or a feeling. A feeling takes about a minute: what
  happened, what you might be feeling (pick up to three words, or type your
  own), what you notice in your body or behavior, and what you might need.
  You can also mark how strong it is from 1 to 10, or leave that alone.
- **Nothing is required and nothing is scored.** Every question can be
  skipped, "Unsure" is a valid answer, and an empty check-in saves. There
  are no streaks, counts or interpretations.
- **Read it back, change one part.** The check-in is shown back in four
  short sections, each with a pencil that reopens only that part. Saved
  check-ins can be reopened the same way, and the reflection can be copied
  as plain text.
- **Search and days in History.** Entries are grouped by day with the time
  on each, a search box looks through everything you wrote, and a filter
  shows all entries, only thoughts, or only feelings.

### Changed

- Thoughts and feelings look different in History: a yellow "Thought" tag
  on white, a warm tinted card with a "Feeling" tag.
- The top bar says "History" and "Your data" in words instead of icons.
- Saving a worksheet is one clear button, "Save on this device".
- Exports and backups include check-ins. Backups made before 1.1 still
  restore.

### Unchanged

- Writing down a thought takes the same number of taps as before, and
  circling and labeling work exactly as they did.
- No account, no server, no analytics, no network requests. Everything
  stays on your device.
